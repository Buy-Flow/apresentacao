Shipping & returns

A simplified presentation of the boutique’s current public policies.

Shipping

Standard orders are generally processed before shipment. Current public policy lists standard delivery of approximately 3–5 business days after shipping, with processing time before dispatch.

Free shipping

Eligible orders of $100 or more within the continental United States qualify for free standard shipping, subject to exclusions in the boutique’s current policy.

Returns

Eligible merchandise must be returned in original condition within the boutique’s stated return window. Returns are for store credit or exchange. Final-sale, seasonal and certain personal-wear categories are excluded.

Local pickup

Local pickup is available in Panama City. Contact the boutique for current pickup timing and storefront hours.
