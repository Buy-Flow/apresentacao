Shop policies

This prototype keeps policy content deliberately high-level so it does not invent legal or operational terms.

Shipping

The boutique currently advertises worldwide shipping. Exact rates, carriers and handling time should come from the live Shopify store.

Local pickup

Local pickup in Panama City is part of the current offer. The production site should display live pickup availability per order.

Returns

Return windows, final-sale exceptions, exchanges and refund timing must match the boutique's active Shopify policy before launch.

Sizing

CHP has described its assortment as carrying sizes XS–XL, while individual garments vary. Product-specific fit notes are the preferred source of truth.

Payments

The current store promotes Shop Pay. A production rebuild can preserve Shopify checkout so payments, taxes, shipping and inventory remain connected.
